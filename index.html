About

I’m Ria, a sophomore studying Political Science and International Relations, and I’m especially interested in AI, government, and foreign policy. I’m curious about how emerging technologies actually get used by public institutions and what happens when policy has to catch up with them.

This quarter, I’m taking POLISCI 158 because I want to better understand how AI is changing politics, institutions, and political decision-making and where its effects might be overstated.

Interests

  • AI governance and policy
  • Foreign policy and diplomacy
  • Government capacity
  • International institutions
  • Public opinion
  • Political economy

Project interests

For the final project, I’d be especially interested in questions like:

  • How is generative AI changing the capacity of governments and public institutions?
  • What determines whether governments adopt AI tools, and what are the political consequences when they do?
  • How might AI affect diplomacy, foreign-policy decision-making, or the way governments communicate with the public?
  • When does AI actually improve government performance, rather than just make existing processes faster?

I’ve previously worked on research around government adoption of generative AI and AI governance, so I’d be excited to build on that work or explore something adjacent. I especially like projects that combine data with a strong substantive question and end in something concrete and useful.
